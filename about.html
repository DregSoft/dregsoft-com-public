About

DregSoft makes practical software for live shows, built by an audio engineer.

DregSoft is the public face for personal projects I’ve made and found useful. Each project exists to solve a problem encountered in my own work.

So far that means MQConduit, a desktop app for macOS and Windows that turns OSC messages, MQTT messages and HTTP requests into actions on a computer; Console Converter, which converts show files between Yamaha RIVAGE PM, Yamaha DM7 and DiGiCo consoles; and Yamaha Channel Reorder, which reorders channels and mixes in Yamaha RIVAGE PM and DM7 show files.

Please note: I am not a professional software developer; I build tools (with help from the community and also AI tools) to solve real problems I encounter in my work as an audio engineer. While every effort is made to ensure the software works correctly, it is provided as-is and may have limitations or bugs. User assumes all risk associated with its use. All projects are open source and covered by their respective licenses.

I hope that you will also find these tools useful, and I welcome any feedback or questions you may have.
